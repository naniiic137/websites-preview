Our story

A legacy of culinary artistry

Founded in 1987 by Chef Jean-Pierre Laurent, La Maison Dorée has been a beacon of French culinary excellence for over three decades. Nestled in the heart of Paris, our dining room blends time-honoured Parisian tradition with quiet contemporary invention.

Every dish tells a story of heritage and season, built on ingredients from trusted purveyors and artisan producers across France.

“Respect the ingredient, honour the technique, and create moments that linger.”
