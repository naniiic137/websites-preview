    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Still stuck? Call +216 70 000 000, 7 days a week, 8:00–21:00.

    How does cash-on-delivery work?

    The courier collects the amount you set when the parcel is handed over. We transfer the money to your bank account or D17 wallet every 48 hours, with a detailed statement. The COD fee is 1.5 DT per parcel.

    What is the latest time to book same-day delivery?

    Book before 13:00 for same-day delivery in Grand Tunis, Sousse and Sfax. Flash (2 hours) is available from 8:00 to 19:00 for trips under 40 km.

    What can’t I send?

    Cash, jewellery above 5,000 DT, weapons, flammable or hazardous materials, live animals and anything prohibited by Tunisian law. Medicines are accepted only from licensed pharmacies.

    What happens if the recipient isn’t home?

    The courier calls the recipient, then tries again the next working day. After two attempts the parcel waits 5 days at the nearest hub before being returned — free of charge on Business plans.

    Is my parcel insured?

    Every parcel is covered up to 100 DT. You can insure it for its full declared value (up to 20,000 DT) for 1% of that value.

    Do you offer an API for my online shop?

    Yes — REST API, webhooks for every status change, and ready-made plug-ins for Shopify, WooCommerce and PrestaShop on Business and Enterprise plans.
