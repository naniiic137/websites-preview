What you get

Built right, not just built.

The same standards you can test in every demo above — included in every project, not sold as extras.

  • Responsive on every screen

    Designed for phones first — where most of your customers are — then laid out properly for tablets and large desktops. No pinching, no sideways scrolling, tap targets sized for thumbs.

  • Fast by default

    Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Compressed WebP images, lazy loading and no heavy frameworks slowing things down.

  • Offline-safe & reliable

    Fonts, images and scripts are self-hosted. No broken images when a third-party link dies, no tracking you didn’t ask for.

  • SEO basics done

    Semantic headings, page titles and descriptions, social-share tags, image alt text and structured data so Google understands your business.

  • Accessible

    Keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast and reduced-motion support — better for every visitor.

  • Features that sell

    Reservations, ordering, carts, bookings, RSVPs, dashboards — interactive features with validation and clear feedback.

  • You own it

    Clean, readable code with no lock-in to a page builder. Host it anywhere, change it any time.

  • Multilingual-ready

    English, French or Arabic — including right-to-left layouts — so every customer reads your site in their language.

How it works

From first message to launch.

  1. 01

    Talk

    You tell me about your business, your customers and what the website needs to do.

  2. 02

    Design

    I propose a look and page structure based on your brand — you review it before any build starts.

  3. 03

    Build

    I build the site and share a live preview link you can open on your own phone and laptop.

  4. 04

    Launch

    We go live on your domain, and I show you how to make simple updates yourself.
