Our story

A private house on the coast, opened to a few guests.

Built as a family villa in the 1920s and restored in 1998, The Azure Palace keeps the scale of a home: just 42 rooms and suites, each facing the water. Mornings start with warm pastries on the terrace; evenings end with the lights of the harbour.
