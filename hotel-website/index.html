Our Story

A Legacy of Timeless Elegance

Nestled along the sun-kissed Mediterranean coast, The Azure Palace has been a beacon of refined hospitality since 1998. Every detail -- from the hand-selected Italian marble in our lobby to the Egyptian cotton linens in each suite -- speaks to our unwavering commitment to excellence.

Our award-winning staff anticipates your every desire, crafting bespoke experiences that transform a simple stay into an unforgettable journey. Whether you seek the tranquility of our infinity pool or the vibrant flavors of our Michelin-inspired restaurant, The Azure Palace promises moments that linger long after departure.
