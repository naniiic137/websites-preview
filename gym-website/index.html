01 — Programs

Five ways to sweat.

Every class is coached, capped in size and scaled to your level — first-timers train next to competitors.

  • Strength & CrossTraining

    Barbells, kettlebells and sleds. Learn the lifts properly, then load them.

  • Boxing

    Pads, bags and footwork. Technique first, then rounds that empty the tank.

  • Spin

    45 minutes, 24 bikes, one playlist. Climb, sprint, recover, repeat.

  • HIIT

    Short, sharp intervals that keep burning long after you leave.

  • Yoga, Pilates & Mobility

    Build the strength and range that keeps the rest of your training pain-free.
