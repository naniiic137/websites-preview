Our love story

Two hearts, one beautiful journey

It began with a borrowed umbrella outside a little bookshop in North Beach. James had the umbrella; Sarah had the last copy of the novel he’d come in for. They agreed to share both — and somehow never stopped sharing everything else.

Eight years, two cities and one very patient golden retriever later, we can’t imagine starting this next chapter without the people who shaped us. You are part of our story, and we would love you to be there when we write its happiest page.

— Sarah & James

How we got here

Moments that mattered

  1. Autumn 2019

    A rainy afternoon

    One umbrella, one novel, two strangers who talked until the bookshop closed.

  2. Summer 2021

    The first adventure

    A week-long road trip down Highway 1 with a map, a playlist and zero plans.

  3. December 2024

Good to know

Details & travel

Dress code

Garden formal. Think soft colours and comfortable shoes — the ceremony is on grass.

Where to stay

A room block is held at The Fairmont until 1 May. Mention “Sarah & James” when booking.

Gifts

Your presence is the greatest gift. If you wish, a honeymoon fund will be at the reception.

Questions you might have

Can I bring a plus-one?

Your invitation names everyone we have reserved a seat for. The RSVP form lets you confirm each guest and their meal.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones! Children are welcome, and a kids’ menu is available — choose it in the RSVP form.

What if it rains?

The ceremony moves under a covered pavilion next to the Rose Garden. We’ll email everyone the morning of the wedding.

Is there transport between venues?

Yes — shuttles leave the Rose Garden at 4:00 pm and return to Union Square hotels from 10:30 pm.
